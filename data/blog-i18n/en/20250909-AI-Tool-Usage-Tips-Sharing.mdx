### 📓 Prompt

<div className="mb-6 rounded-xl border border-indigo-200 bg-gradient-to-r from-indigo-50 to-purple-50 p-6 dark:border-indigo-800 dark:from-indigo-900/20 dark:to-purple-900/20">
  <h4 className="mb-4 text-lg font-semibold text-indigo-800 dark:text-indigo-200">🎯 What is a prompt?</h4>
  <div className="mb-4 text-indigo-700 dark:text-indigo-300">The word “prompt” is actually like the first thing you say when you’re having a conversation with someone. What you say and how you say it will influence the other person’s response.</div>

  <div className="text-indigo-700 dark:text-indigo-300">

    to determine how to respond to you.

</div>

### 🔑 A Few Tips for Conversing with LLMs

<div className="space-y-6 mb-8">
  <div className="bg-white dark:bg-gray-800 p-6 rounded-lg shadow-md border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-700">
    <div className="flex items-center mb-4">
      <div className="w-10 h-10 bg-red-100 dark:bg-red-900 rounded-full flex items-center justify-center mr-4">
        <span className="text-red-600 dark:text-red-400 font-bold">1</span>
      </div>

      <h4 className="text-lg font-semibold text-gray-800 dark:text-gray-200">Be Specific, Not Vague</h4>
    </div>

    <div className="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-4">
      <div className="bg-red-50 dark:bg-red-900/20 p-4 rounded-lg border-l-4 border-red-500">
        <h5 className="font-semibold text-red-800 dark:text-red-200 mb-2">❌ Vague Question</h5>
        <div className="text-red-700 dark:text-red-300 text-sm">
          "Write an article for me" &lt;br/&gt;
          The model has absolutely no idea what you want, so the results it produces are not what you're looking for at all.
        </div>
      </div>

      <div className="bg-green-50 dark:bg-green-900/20 p-4 rounded-lg border-l-4 border-green-500">
        <h5 className="font-semibold text-green-800 dark:text-green-200 mb-2">✅ Specific Questions</h5>
        <div className="text-green-700 dark:text-green-300 text-sm">
          "Write an 800-character article on the use of AI tools, in the style of a technical blog." &lt;br/&gt;
          The result may not immediately match what you have in mind, but it is within your control.
        </div>
      </div>

    </div>
  </div>

<div className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200 bg-white p-6 shadow-md dark:border-gray-700 dark:bg-gray-800">
  <div className="mb-4 flex items-center">
    <div className="mr-4 flex h-10 w-10 items-center justify-center rounded-full bg-blue-100 dark:bg-blue-900">
      <span className="font-bold text-blue-600 dark:text-blue-400">2</span>
    </div>

    <h4 className="text-lg font-semibold text-gray-800 dark:text-gray-200">Step-by-Step Questions</h4>
  </div>

  <div className="rounded-lg bg-blue-50 p-4 dark:bg-blue-900/20">
    &lt;ul className="space-y-2 text-blue-700 dark:text-blue-300"&gt;
      &lt;li&gt;First, have the student “break down the problem,” then have them “write out the answer.”&lt;/li&gt;
      &lt;li&gt;Just like when doing complex calculations, you first work through a rough draft before writing down the final steps&lt;/li&gt;
    &lt;/ul&gt;
  </div>
</div>

<div className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200 bg-white p-6 shadow-md dark:border-gray-700 dark:bg-gray-800">
  <div className="mb-4 flex items-center">
    <div className="mr-4 flex h-10 w-10 items-center justify-center rounded-full bg-purple-100 dark:bg-purple-900">
      <span className="font-bold text-purple-600 dark:text-purple-400">3</span>
    </div>

    <h4 className="text-lg font-semibold text-gray-800 dark:text-gray-200">Set a Role or Identity</h4>
  </div>

  <div className="rounded-lg bg-purple-50 p-4 dark:bg-purple-900/20">
    <div className="space-y-3">
      <div className="rounded border bg-white p-3 dark:bg-gray-800">
        <div className="text-sm text-purple-700 dark:text-purple-300">&lt;strong&gt;Example 1: &lt;/strong&gt;"Pretend you're Socrates and guide me through a line of reasoning using questions and answers."</div>
      </div>

      <div className="rounded border bg-white p-3 dark:bg-gray-800">
        <div className="text-sm text-purple-700 dark:text-purple-300">
          &lt;strong&gt;Example 2: &lt;/strong&gt;
          "You are a senior systems architect. Please help me evaluate this technical design proposal."
        </div>
      </div>

    </div>

    <div className="mt-3 rounded bg-purple-100 p-3 dark:bg-purple-800">
      <div className="text-sm text-purple-800 dark:text-purple-200">👉 This way, the model will adopt a certain “personality pattern,” resulting in a more natural response style.</div>
    </div>

  </div>
</div>

<div className="rounded-lg border border-gray-200 bg-white p-6 shadow-md dark:border-gray-700 dark:bg-gray-800">
  <div className="mb-4 flex items-center">
    <div className="mr-4 flex h-10 w-10 items-center justify-center rounded-full bg-orange-100 dark:bg-orange-900">
      <span className="font-bold text-orange-600 dark:text-orange-400">4</span>
    </div>

    <h4 className="text-lg font-semibold text-gray-800 dark:text-gray-200">Multiple Rounds of Iterative Dialogue</h4>
  </div>

  <div className="rounded-lg bg-orange-50 p-4 dark:bg-orange-900/20">
    &lt;ul className="space-y-2 text-orange-700 dark:text-orange-300"&gt;
      &lt;li&gt;
        Don’t expect a “perfect first draft.” Start by getting a rough draft, then say, “Make it a little more concise” or “Try a more relaxed tone.”
      &lt;/li&gt;
      &lt;li&gt;The real strength of LLMs lies in “collaborative editing,” not in producing a perfect draft on the first try.&lt;/li&gt;
    &lt;/ul&gt;
  </div>
</div>

  <div className="bg-white dark:bg-gray-800 p-6 rounded-lg shadow-md border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-700">
